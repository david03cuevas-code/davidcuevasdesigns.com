FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Timber Architecture & Engineering FAQ

Everything you need to know about our custom cedar pergolas, carports, and St. Petersburg/Clearwater hurricane compliance.

Custom 8x8 Western Red Cedar pergolas built by David Cuevas Designs in St. Petersburg, Clearwater, and South Tampa start from $19,000 to $42,000+ depending on footprint size, roof structure, custom joinery, and engineering requirements. All builds include stamped PE wind-load engineering, city permitting, 3+ foot deep concrete pier footings, marine-grade Teak Oil treatment, and master installation.

Traditional metal brackets rust and corrode in Pinellas County coastal salt air, trapping moisture that causes wood rot. Japanese timber joinery uses precision mortise-and-tenon and proprietary 45° Tri-Axis compound joints where wood locks into wood. Under 160+ MPH hurricane wind loads, wood fibers compress together, increasing joint rigidity without relying on vulnerable screws.

David Cuevas Designs specializes in custom heavy-timber carports engineered to complement historic architectural styles in neighborhoods like Snell Isle, Old Northeast St. Pete, Belleair, Hyde Park, and Davis Islands. We handle 100% of municipal permitting, HOA submittals, and Florida building code compliance.

Western Red Cedar contains natural tannins and oils that resist moisture, insects, and decay. Applying marine-grade Teak Oil penetrates deep into the grain, protecting the timber from intense Florida UV rays and tropical downpours while accentuating the rich natural grain without peeling like surface stains.

Because David pre-cuts, mortises, and test-fits 90% of the heavy timber structure off-site in his studio shop, on-site installation at your estate usually takes only 1 to 2 days, minimizing noise and disruption to your property.
